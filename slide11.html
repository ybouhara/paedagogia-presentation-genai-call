Environmental Considerations

We believe educational innovation should include environmental responsibility. Here's how we're approaching this challenge:

Responsible Data Hosting

We plan to prioritize local Belgian and European data hosting solutions that align with both privacy and environmental values.

Our Approach

Looking for GDPR-compliant hosting options that utilize renewable energy where available, balancing data sovereignty with environmental impact.

Energy-Efficient AI

Rather than training entirely new models, we aim to fine-tune existing AI technologies to significantly reduce computational resource needs.

Our Approach

This methodological choice could reduce energy usage compared to building models from scratch, supporting climate goals while maintaining performance.

Privacy-Respecting Design

Our commitment to privacy by design includes prioritizing data minimization and local processing where possible, which has environmental benefits too.

Our Approach

Local-first architectures reduce constant data transfer needs, decreasing network energy consumption while enhancing student data protection.

Our Commitment

We're exploring and implementing these approaches while recognizing that sustainable technology is an evolving journey. We'll continue to seek improvements as technologies and best practices advance.
