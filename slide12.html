Regional Contributions

PaedagogIA hopes to contribute to the Brussels ecosystem while addressing educational challenges.

Ethical EdTech Development

Working toward responsible applications of AI for practical educational challenges, supporting Brussels' growing reputation in ethical technology innovation.

Brussels' multilingual, multicultural character provides a valuable testing environment for educational tools with broader applicability.

Collaborative Innovation

Building bridges between educators, technologists, and policymakers, while engaging with local institutions like the AI for the Common Good Institute (FARI).

Fostering a community of practice that connects academic institutions, schools, tech companies, and public authorities to collaborate on educational solutions.

Knowledge Economy Support

If successful, our project could contribute modestly to the region's knowledge economy through specialized roles in educational AI and learning analytics.

Potential to help connect educational institutions with the growing EdTech sector to develop and retain skilled professionals in the region.
Supporting Brussels' vision to be a leader in technology that benefits society
