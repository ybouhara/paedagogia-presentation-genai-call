The Daily Challenge

Teachers face three major challenges:

1. Lack of precise diagnostic tools

Identifying individual knowledge gaps with precision is a significant challenge, especially when these gaps date back several years.

2. Generic remediation resources

Available tools are not personalized to each student's specific needs, limiting the effectiveness of interventions.

3. Compounding difficulties

Unresolved gaps create difficulties that accumulate over time, compromising future learning.

Example: Secondary school equation failure often traces back to primary school concepts that were never mastered.
