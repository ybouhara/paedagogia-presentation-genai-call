The Problem in Numbers

Academic delays and failures

  • 47.8% academic delay by final year of secondary education
  • Retention jumps from 4.7% (1st primary) to 16.6% (3rd secondary)
  • 60% of math difficulties stem from pre-CEB gaps

Socioeconomic impact

  • Disadvantaged students are 2.5x more likely to experience academic delay
  • 15-point gap in math scores between low vs. high socioeconomic schools

CEB (88.5% pass rate hides reality)

  • 10% score below 50/100 in mathematics
  • Early success masks issues emerging later

Key insight

Success rates can be misleading indicators of true educational progress. Many students pass tests while carrying unaddressed knowledge gaps that limit their potential.

Source: Education Indicators 2024 - FWB
